Our Story

Passion poured
into every cup.

Atti & Co was born from a simple belief — that everyone deserves access to exceptional, thoughtfully crafted coffee, wherever they are. What started as a single van and an obsession with specialty beans has grown into one of the region's most trusted mobile coffee experiences.

We source our beans from renowned roasters, dial in every espresso with precision, and bring genuine hospitality to every event we serve. Whether it's your morning market stop or the most important day of your life, we show up ready to make it memorable.

FAQ

Common questions.

Have more questions? We'd love to chat. Reach out and we'll get back to you within a few hours.

What areas do you service? +

We're based in Sydney and service all of Greater Sydney, the Hunter Valley, Central Coast, and Blue Mountains. We'll travel further for the right event — just ask us about travel fees.

How far in advance should I book? +

We recommend booking at least 4–8 weeks in advance, especially for weekends and popular seasons. We occasionally have last-minute availability, so it's always worth reaching out.

What does a typical package include? +

Each package includes a fully-equipped mobile coffee van, trained barista staff, specialty coffee beans, premium milk (dairy & alternatives), compostable cups, and setup/pack-down. Packages are tailored to your event size and duration.

Do you offer dairy-free and alternative milks? +

Absolutely. We always carry oat, almond, and soy milk as standard. Just let us know your guests' dietary needs and we'll make sure everyone's covered.

Can the van be customised to match our event branding? +

Yes! We can incorporate your brand colours, add custom signage, and style the van to match your event aesthetic. This is especially popular for corporate events and weddings.

What's the minimum booking duration? +

Our minimum booking is 2 hours for most events. For smaller or private functions, please get in touch and we'll do our best to accommodate you.
