Our story

Brewed with
intention.

Atti & Co began as a simple idea: great coffee shouldn't be tied to four walls. Our founder Atticus spent years training under Melbourne's finest roasters before loading up a vintage van and taking the craft to the streets.

Every cup is made to order, pulled on a commercial La Marzocco, using freshly roasted single-origin beans sourced directly from farmers we know by name.
