Our Story

Born from a love of great coffee.

Atti & Co started with a simple belief: that the best coffee experiences shouldn't be confined to four walls. We took our passion for specialty coffee, loaded it into a beautifully fitted van, and brought it to the people.

From our very first market stall to now serving hundreds of events each year, we've stayed true to what matters — exceptional coffee, genuine hospitality, and that warm feeling of a perfect cup shared with good company.

We partner with local roasters, use sustainably sourced milk and ingredients, and train every barista to the highest standard. Whether you're hosting fifty guests or five thousand, we show up ready to impress.

FAQ

Good questions, honest answers.

How far in advance should I book? ▾
We recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks ahead for corporate events and 3–6 months for weddings. That said, we do take last-minute bookings depending on availability — don't hesitate to reach out!
What's included in a booking? ▾
Every booking includes our fully equipped coffee van, experienced barista(s), all coffee equipment, specialty beans, milk, and consumables (cups, lids, stirrers). We handle setup and pack-down so you don't have to.
Do you cater for non-coffee drinkers? ▾
Absolutely! We offer a range of non-coffee options including matcha lattes, hot chocolate, chai, and various cold drinks. We can also accommodate all dietary requirements including dairy-free alternatives.
How much space does the van need? ▾
Our van requires approximately 6m × 3m of flat, accessible space. We'll discuss the specifics of your venue during the booking process to make sure everything runs smoothly on the day.
Do you travel outside Melbourne? ▾
Yes! We service all of Victoria and can travel interstate for large events. Travel fees may apply depending on distance. Contact us and we'll work something out.
